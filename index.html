⚡ Platform Features

Everything MasjidBD offers for mosques, communities and government across Bangladesh.

🕌

Mosque Directory

All mosques in Bangladesh searchable by location, district and facilities.

🕐

Prayer Times

Accurate prayer times for all 64 districts with live countdown and iqamah schedules.

💚

Donation Platform

Transparent fundraising for mosque renovation, relief and education campaigns.

📊

Role-Based Dashboard

Dedicated portals for members, imams, volunteers, finance officers, admins and government.

🗺️

Interactive Map

Leaflet-powered map showing all mosques with filters by district and facilities.

🤖

MasjidBot AI

AI assistant powered by Gemini for Islamic Q&A, prayer guidance and community support.

🏛️ Government Portal

Official portal for Ministry of Religious Affairs and district government officers managing mosque oversight.

📋 Mosque Registration Status

View and approve mosque registration applications from all 64 districts. Currently 12 pending applications.

📊 National Statistics

Aggregate data on mosque attendance, fund utilisation and community programs across Bangladesh.

🔐 Government Login

Authorised Ministry of Religious Affairs officers log in with their government credentials.

🕌 Register Your Mosque

Add your mosque to Bangladesh's national directory. Free for all mosques.

Registration is free. Your mosque will appear on the map and directory after verification.
